From Dumpsters to Farmhouse Charm: Trotters Trail in Cocoa, FL

February 24, 2027 — Cocoa, FL

Not every project starts as a house. Trotters Trail in Cocoa began as an industrial site with dumpsters, debris, and a building that most buyers would have walked away from. That's exactly the kind of challenge we take on.

The Before

February 2025: green dumpsters, exposed concrete, and a brown industrial building that had seen better days. The property needed more than a renovation — it needed vision.

The Cleanup and Conversion

  • Exterior cleanup: Removed debris, dumpsters, and graded the site
  • Building overhaul: Converted the industrial structure to a white farmhouse-style home
  • Landscaping: New lawn, mulch beds, and curb appeal
  • Pergola: Added outdoor living space to the front patio

The Result

By April 2025, the property was a completely different place. The twilight MLS photos show a white ranch with a wooden pergola, manicured lawn, and a front porch that invites you in.
