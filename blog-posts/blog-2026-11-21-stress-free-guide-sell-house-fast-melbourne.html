The Problems with Listing on the Traditional Market

When you list your Melbourne home with a real estate agent, you are entering a process full of variables and potential delays. First, there is the preparation phase. Most agents will recommend repairs, painting, decluttering, and staging to make your home appealing to buyers. These improvements can cost thousands of dollars and weeks of effort.

Next comes the showing schedule. You must keep your home spotless and be ready to leave at a moment's notice for private showings and open houses. If you have children, pets, or a demanding job, this disruption adds significant stress to your daily life.

Even after you accept an offer, the closing process is fraught with obstacles. The buyer's lender requires an appraisal, which can come in low and derail the deal. Home inspections often reveal issues that lead to renegotiation or costly repairs. According to industry data, roughly twenty percent of pending home sales fall through before closing. If you are on a tight timeline, starting over with a new buyer is a nightmare scenario.

Finally, do not forget the costs. Agent commissions in Florida typically total six percent of the sale price. On a three-hundred-thousand-dollar home, that is eighteen thousand dollars out of your pocket. Add in closing costs, repair concessions, and holding costs like mortgage, utilities, and insurance while your home sits on the market, and your net proceeds shrink dramatically.
