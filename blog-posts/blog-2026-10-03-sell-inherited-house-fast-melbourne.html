Navigating Probate and Legal Requirements in Florida

Before you can sell an inherited house in Florida, the property typically must go through probate. This legal process validates the will, settles outstanding debts, and transfers ownership to the rightful heirs. In Florida, probate can take anywhere from six months to over a year, depending on the complexity of the estate and whether any disputes arise.

However, not all inherited properties require full probate. Florida offers a simplified probate process for smaller estates, and properties held in a trust or with transfer-on-death deeds can often bypass probate entirely. If the deceased had a properly structured estate plan, you might be able to sell the house much sooner than you think.

Common Questions About Selling Inherited Houses in Melbourne

Do all heirs need to agree to sell? Yes, all legal owners must consent to the sale. If the property has passed through probate and multiple heirs own it jointly, everyone must sign off. If heirs disagree, a partition action through the courts may be necessary, though this is rare when everyone understands the financial benefits of a quick sale.

What if the house has a mortgage? Mortgages don't disappear when the owner passes away. The estate or heirs typically become responsible for continued payments. A cash sale allows you to pay off the remaining mortgage balance at closing, with any surplus going to the heirs.

Are there tax implications? Inherited properties receive a stepped-up basis, meaning capital gains taxes are calculated from the property's value at the time of death rather than the original purchase price. This often significantly reduces or eliminates capital gains liability. However, we recommend consulting with a tax professional for advice specific to your situation.

What about personal belongings? You decide what happens to the contents of the home. Many families sort through belongings before contacting us, but if you need to sell quickly, we can handle cleanout after purchase or work with you to donate usable items to local Melbourne charities.
