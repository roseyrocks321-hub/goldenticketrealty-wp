Commercial to Residential: Sunflower Dr in Port St John, FL

March 10, 2027 — Port St John, FL

Some of the most interesting projects don't start as homes at all. Sunflower Dr in Port St John was a commercial food-prep space with stainless steel surfaces, industrial lighting, and a layout designed for production — not living.

The Starting Point

January 2024: commercial kitchen equipment, exposed brick, utility rooms, and a basement-like mechanical space. The property had good square footage and solid construction, but it needed a complete rethink.

The Conversion

  • Full gut: Removed all commercial equipment and industrial finishes
  • Kitchen: Built a modern residential kitchen with white shaker cabinets, quartz countertops, and stainless appliances
  • Living spaces: Converted open commercial areas into defined bedrooms and living rooms
  • Systems: Updated electrical, plumbing, and HVAC for residential use

The Result

By May 2024, the transformation was complete. What was once a commercial kitchen is now a modern residential home with clean lines, updated finishes, and move-in-ready appeal.
