The Traditional Route: Why It Is Not Always the Best Fit

Listing with a real estate agent works well for sellers who have time, money for repairs, and patience for the process. But for many homeowners, the traditional route comes with serious drawbacks:

  • Upfront costs: Repairs, cleaning, landscaping, and staging can cost thousands before you ever list.
  • Agent commissions: Expect to pay 5% to 6% of the sale price in commissions.
  • Buyer contingencies: Inspections, appraisals, and financing approvals can derail a deal at the last minute.
  • Showings and open houses: Keeping your home spotless for weeks or months is exhausting.
  • Closing delays: Even after you accept an offer, it can take 30 to 45 days to close.

If you are in a situation where time is money, every day your house sits unsold costs you. That is where a direct cash sale changes everything.
