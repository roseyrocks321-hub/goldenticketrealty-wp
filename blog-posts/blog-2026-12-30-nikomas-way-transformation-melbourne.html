From Framing to Finish: 362 Nikomas Way in Melbourne Beach, FL

December 30, 2026 — Melbourne Beach, FL

Some properties tell a story before you ever step inside. 362 Nikomas Way in Melbourne Beach, FL is one of them. What started as raw framing and exposed studs became a modern, move-in-ready home with an open-concept great room, updated kitchen, and a master suite overlooking the water.

The Starting Point

When we first walked the property in May 2025, the interior was down to the bones. Exposed wall studs, unfinished ceilings, and a layout that needed rethinking. The vision was clear: create a functional, contemporary space that maximized the home's waterfront potential.

The Transformation

Over the next ten months, the space came together:

  • Kitchen: New island with quartz countertops, shaker-style gray cabinetry, and stainless appliances
  • Great room: Open floor plan connecting dining, kitchen, and living areas with wood-style plank flooring
  • Staircase: Modern open-riser design with vertical slat railings
  • Master suite: Spacious bedroom with en-suite bath and walk-in closet

The Result

By March 2026, the property was fully finished and photographed. The twilight exterior shots show a home that's unrecognizable from where we started — clean lines, modern finishes, and curb appeal that matches the interior quality.
