The Traditional Route: Listing with a Real Estate Agent

Before you decide how to sell, it helps to understand what the traditional route actually involves. When you list your Melbourne home with a real estate agent, the process usually starts with a consultation and a comparative market analysis. Your agent will recommend repairs, upgrades, and staging to make the home more appealing. You will need to keep the property clean and available for showings, open houses, and inspections.

Once you accept an offer, the buyer typically needs 30 to 45 days to secure financing. During that time, the lender orders an appraisal and the buyer may request repairs based on the inspection report. If the appraisal comes in low or the buyer's financing falls through, you are back at square one. Even in a best-case scenario, a traditional sale in Melbourne takes 45 to 90 days from listing to closing.

For homeowners who have time and a market-ready property, this route can yield a higher sale price. But for those who need speed, certainty, or simplicity, the traditional process often creates more stress than it is worth. Commissions alone typically cost 5 to 6 percent of the sale price, plus closing costs, repair concessions, and holding costs like utilities, insurance, and mortgage payments while you wait.
