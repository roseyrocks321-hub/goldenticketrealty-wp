The Challenges of Selling an Inherited House in Melbourne

Selling an inherited property is rarely as simple as putting a "For Sale" sign in the front yard. Melbourne homeowners who inherit real estate often face several unique challenges:

Understanding Probate House Sales in Florida

Florida's probate process can be intimidating, but understanding the basics helps you make informed decisions. Probate is the court-supervised process of distributing a deceased person's assets. In Florida, there are two main types:

  • Formal Administration: Required for larger estates or when disputes exist. This process typically takes six to twelve months.
  • Summary Administration: Available for smaller estates or when the decedent has been deceased for more than two years. This streamlined process can take just a few weeks to a few months.

Once the court appoints a personal representative (executor), that person has the authority to sell the property. However, if multiple heirs are involved, everyone must agree on the sale terms. Tax Considerations When Selling Inherited Property in Florida

Florida does not have a state inheritance tax, which is good news for heirs. However, federal tax rules still apply. Inherited property receives a "stepped-up basis," meaning the home's tax basis is adjusted to its fair market value at the time of the owner's death. This often reduces or eliminates capital gains taxes when you sell.

Still, every situation is different. We recommend consulting with a tax professional or estate attorney to understand your specific obligations. Golden Ticket Realty is happy to work with your advisors to ensure the sale proceeds smoothly.
